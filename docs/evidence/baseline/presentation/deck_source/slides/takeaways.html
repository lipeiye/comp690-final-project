Takeaways

1

Which electrodes, and how many, matter more than how the weights are stored.

2

Sixteen task-chosen electrodes kept full accuracy; modelling redundancy added nothing over relevance.

3

INT8 and pruning shrink bytes, not time: on this CPU, kernel coverage decided latency.

Thank you · questions welcome

To sum up. First, for a decoder this small, the number and choice of electrodes is the real lever: it sets the compute, the activation memory and the latency, and it is also the hardware cost. Second, sixteen electrodes picked by any task-aware rule were enough on a new day, but the redundancy term did not beat simply ranking by relevance. Third, INT8 and pruning were free in accuracy and shrank the weights, but the weights were never the bottleneck, and on this CPU INT8 was even slower because of one missing integer kernel.
