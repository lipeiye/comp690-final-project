Not a new algorithm: the rule is an mRMR-style heuristic

Peiye Li · COMP 690 · 3 · full references in the proposal; Q-EEGNet: SMARTCOMP 2020; MI-BMInet: arXiv 2203.14592

Compact and embedded EEG decoding is well studied. EEGNet is the compact network I use. Q-EEGNet already ran an 8-bit EEGNet on a low-power microcontroller with about a 0.4-point accuracy loss, and MI-BMInet combined electrode selection from learned spatial weights with 8-bit deployment on microcontrollers. My selection rule is an mRMR-style relevance-minus-redundancy heuristic, not a new algorithm. What this project adds is the controlled comparison: four selection rules at the same electrode count, with the same decoder and training budget, each crossed with INT8, under a test day that stayed locked until the plan was frozen. That design is what lets me say which choice actually matters, including a negative result and its cause.
