Choosing the electrodes

next = argmax relevance(c) − 0.5 × mean |ρ(c, chosen)|

Proposed rule

  • Relevance: Fisher score of mu (8–13 Hz) and beta (13–30 Hz) log power, scaled 0–1
  • Redundancy: |Spearman ρ| of that log power across trials
  • Training trials only; keep 16 or 8 of 22

Controls, same count

  • Relevance only (penalty 0)
  • Spatial-weight norm of the trained 22-electrode EEGNet
  • Three fixed random subsets
  • All 22 electrodes as the reference

The proposed rule is an mRMR-style heuristic, not a new algorithm. It starts from the most relevant electrode and then keeps adding the electrode whose relevance, minus half its average correlation with the electrodes already chosen, is highest. Relevance-only is the same rule with the penalty set to zero, so comparing the two isolates the redundancy term. The weight-norm control is an adaptation inspired by MI-BMInet.
