Peiye Li · COMP 690 · 13 · status on 9 November 2026 · github.com/lipeiye/comp690-final-project

Against the proposal timeline, every experimental phase is complete: the baseline, the export and INT8 operator checks, the selection rules and validation, the FP32 and INT8 comparisons, the bounded pruning study, evaluation of all nine participants, uncertainty estimates, efficiency measurements and the replay demo. The analysis plan was frozen on November 3, before day 2 was scored. The fallback in the proposal, quantization-aware training, was not needed because INT8 lost at most half a point. Two things went beyond the plan: an equal-fine-tuning pruning control, added after the test results and labeled post-test, and a public repository with all code and model weights. What remains is the late-November write-up, which is on schedule.
